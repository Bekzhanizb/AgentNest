# Code of Conduct

## Our expectations

Everyone participating in AgentNest's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces should be treated with respect, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or ability. Be constructive, explain technical disagreements, credit
others' work, and respect privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.
Repeated disruption or retaliation against someone reporting a concern is also
unacceptable. These expectations apply when someone represents the project in
other public spaces.

## Reporting and privacy

The owner has not designated a private conduct contact in this foundation change.
Use an owner-designated private channel once one is listed here. Until then, you
may open a minimal issue asking for a private contact, without naming affected
people or including incident details. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.
GitHub's own reporting tools remain available for violations of its policies.

Publishing a private conduct contact is an owner setup task on the
[roadmap](ROADMAP.md); this document does not invent an email address or promise
a staffed reporting service.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask a participant to stop, issue a
warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They should choose
an action proportionate to severity and repeated behavior, explain it privately
where feasible, and protect the reporter's identity and sensitive evidence.
Maintainers involved in a report should recuse themselves where another uninvolved
maintainer is available. An appeal can go to an uninvolved maintainer through the
designated private channel when available. No independent appeals body or response
SLA is currently established.
